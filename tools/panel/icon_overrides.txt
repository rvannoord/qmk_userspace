# Hand-finished layer icons.
#
# A threshold cut is clean but it is not a draughtsman: it leaves a stroke a pixel thin
# where the source's was a hair under the boundary, chips a keycap, and breaks symmetry
# that the source had and the eye expects. These grids are the repair, applied by
# gen_layer_icons.py *after* the reduction - which is why the generated .c stays
# generated and nothing here is a hand edit of it.
#
# An icon with no entry here is used exactly as the reduction produced it.
#
# "#" is a set bit: a lit pixel of the glyph.
# Coordinates are band coordinates - band row 0 is panel row 8.
#
# What each entry fixes, so a future change knows what it would undo:
#
#   TAP     the five keycaps were chewed to 1px and two vanished; the frame was 3 rows
#           on top and 2 at the bottom. Now five uniform 2x3 caps and a 3px border.
#   MEDIA   the gear's top teeth differed from its bottom teeth. Mirrored about row 20.
#   SYM     the braces disagreed with themselves in both axes - a 1px-short tip, two
#           dropped columns, and shoulders of 8px against 4px. One quadrant, mirrored.
#   NUM     the x had 1px tips and no top-left terminal at all; the + was ragged and
#           off-centre; the = bars differed by a pixel. All three re-struck on the
#           same 8x8 box, centres on 57.5/25.5.
#   FUN     the sigma's top bar was 5 rows and its bottom 4. Both are 4 rows of 20.
#   NAV     the four arms should be one arrow stamped four times and were not: the up
#           and down heads reduced solid, the left and right as open chevrons with
#           mismatched barbs. Now 4-fold symmetric, 0 mirror breaks.
#   MOUSE   the 3px body wall pinched to 2px at three rows on both sides. The 2px
#           centre divider is deliberate: an interior line should be lighter than an
#           outline.
#   BUTTON  the page's right wall pinched for its last row, and the top-right corner
#           was squarer than the top-left.
